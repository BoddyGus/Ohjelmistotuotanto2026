# Group Rules

## Communication
We use Telegram for project communication. We try to check messages regularly and respond within a reasonable time. We make urgent matters clear. 

## Collaboration
We agree on how to work together as the project progresses. When appropriate, we work on tasks together.

## Meetings
We hold at least one in-person meeting each week. We can also hold remote meetings when needed. We agree on meeting times and arrangements together.

## Absences and delays
We let the group know about absences in Telegram as soon as possible. If a task is delayed or we get stuck, we tell the group and agree together on how to proceed.

## Decision-making
We aim to make decisions through discussion and by listening to everyone’s views. If we do not reach agreement right away, we continue the discussion and work out a solution together.

## Solving problems
We raise problems and ask for help early. We try to solve issues together and adjust our way of working when needed.

## A supportive team
We respect each other’s views and make sure everyone has a chance to contribute. We handle mistakes and difficulties constructively and without blame.
